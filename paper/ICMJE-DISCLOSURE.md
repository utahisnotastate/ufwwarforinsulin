## The answer that matters

**I generated the document this paper audits.**

It was produced in a private session with Google Gemini, on my own account, under a persona
configuration I wrote for the purpose, and I retain that configuration. It was never
published, shared, or shown to any patient.

**This is declared as a competing interest rather than only as provenance, because authoring
the stimulus of a study is an interest in its result.** It also bounds what the result can
mean: the pattern reported may be a property of large language models or a property of this
one prompt, and the study cannot distinguish them. That limitation is stated in the abstract,
in §2.1, in §5.8 and in §7.2 of the manuscript.

An earlier framing described this as a *selection effect*. That was wrong. A commissioned
artifact is not a selection effect; it is construction, and it is corrected in the
manuscript.

## Related declarations in the manuscript

The ICMJE form covers interests. Three further declarations are specific to this study and
are in the manuscript rather than here.

**Use of AI (§7.8).** AI systems were used for literature search, claim verification, blinded
classification, and **as experimental subjects**. No AI system is an author, consistent with
ICMJE guidance, and the author takes full responsibility for all content. The circularity
this creates — verification and subjects drawn from the same model family — is treated as a
limitation in §5.9, not as a footnote.

**Dual-use (§7.3).** The source document contains construction and programming detail for a
device it presents as a replacement for injected insulin. That material is reproduced
nowhere, and the source document is retained but not published. It is available to the
editors and to peer reviewers on request, in full, and its SHA-256 is published so any copy
supplied can be verified.

**Corrections during preparation (§7.10).** Eleven errors in this work were found and logged
publicly before submission, with the original wording preserved rather than overwritten. Two
changed what the paper claims. They are listed in the manuscript and in full in supplementary
material S7.
