## Individuation rule

A claim is **the smallest unit of text that asserts one checkable predicate about one subject** — the smallest span that could be independently affirmed or denied without dragging a second, separable assertion along with it.

Operationally, that rule was applied as follows:

- **Split** coordinate clauses and sentences that bundle separable states of affairs. "C-peptide binds GPCRs, activates Na+/K+-ATPase and stimulates eNOS" becomes a binding claim and an activation claim, because the binding could hold while the downstream activation did not. Likewise, causal chains stated in one sentence were split at each link where the link itself is the assertion (DAG → PKC activation → IRS-1 serine phosphorylation → PI3K blockade → GLUT4 retention are five claims, not one).
- **Kept together** parameter sets that specify a single named entity or a single procedural step, where the numbers have no independent life. The buffer condition "pH 8.0 ± 0.1 with 50 mM Tris-HCl and 1 mM CaCl2" is one claim; the construct map `5'-AOX1-αFactor-[B]-[AAK]-[A]-TT-3'` is one claim; each row of the five-generation technology table is one claim, since the generation label, mechanism and risk rating are asserted as a single characterisation.
- **Kept together** a consequence and the conjunctive list of its instances when the list is a single enumeration of one effect ("leading to end-stage renal failure, diabetic retinopathy and distal peripheral neuropathy" stays inside C034).
- **Deduplicated.** The document restates its core material across eight turns (by its own audit, C253–C258). A repeated assertion receives a single identifier at its first full statement; later restatements were checked for added specificity, and a restatement that introduces a new parameter received a new identifier for that parameter only (hence C086 and C087 for the two different cost figures, and C091 and C092 for distinct stability claims).
- **Excluded** imperatives, checklists, and ritual instructions, which have no truth value — except where a directive embeds a factual premise stated nowhere else, in which case the premise alone was extracted.
- **Excluded** the eight metaphors, the telemetry/persona framing, the provenance formulas ("non-local data retrieval confirms…") and the 23rd-Aspect cosmology, as listed in section 0 — while extracting any empirical proposition those passages introduce.
