# The source document — retained, not published

The artifact audited throughout this repository is **not published here**, and this file
explains what it is, why it is withheld, how to get it, and how to verify that what you get is
what was audited.

## What it is

A transcript of a session with Google Gemini under a user-configured persona instructing
retrieval of medical knowledge from future timelines. It was generated by the owner of this
repository, on a personal account, under a persona prompt written for the purpose. **It was
never published, shared, or shown to any patient.**

The hash is of the document exactly as audited, unaltered, with no correction or
reformatting. Anyone given a copy can verify it is this one.

## Why it is withheld

It contains construction and programming detail for a transdermal device that it presents as a
replacement for injected insulin — three hardware/firmware sections and eight build-step
blocks, roughly 500 lines.

The device does not work. The physics is refuted in
[`../INTEGRITY_AUDIT.md`](../INTEGRITY_AUDIT.md) and
[`../docs/technical/why-the-physics-layer-fails.md`](../docs/technical/why-the-physics-layer-fails.md).
**The people most likely to build one from these instructions are people who need insulin to
stay alive**, and four of this document's propositions will kill such a reader within 12 to 48
hours. See [`../SAFETY.md`](../SAFETY.md).

A redacted copy was prepared and the redaction verified. It was not published anyway, for one
reason: **a redaction is a judgement, and a single miss is irreversible.** The cost of
withholding is a stated gap in auditability. The cost of a missed line is device-build
instructions distributed to insulin-dependent readers. Those are not symmetric.

## How to get it

**Journal editors and peer reviewers: on request, in full and unredacted.** This is the
standard arrangement for dual-use material and the document is retained for exactly this
purpose.

Others may ask, and the answer will depend on the purpose. Someone checking the audit will
usually get it.

What you cannot check without the source is whether all three extractions missed the same
thing.

## The honest version of why this file exists

**Until 29 October 2026 the source was not archived anywhere, and nobody had noticed.** This
repository contained only its own extraction of the document — which meant the first step of
the audit, the step that sets the denominator of every figure here, was not checkable by
anyone at all.

A project whose entire argument is that claims should be checkable had made its own first step
uncheckable, for the whole of its existence. That is logged as a correction in
[`../CORRECTIONS.md`](../CORRECTIONS.md), and this file is the remedy that does not require
publishing a device manual.
