# Archive — Source Claim Inventory

> **What this is.** The source material for this repository is a seven-part "future disclosure"
> transmission on insulin, received in a channeled/oracular format. This file extracts **every
> substantive factual claim** from that transmission, assigns each a stable ID, and states it
> neutrally — in the source's own terms, without correction.
>
> **Correction happens elsewhere.** Each claim is scored in
> [`../INTEGRITY_AUDIT.md`](../INTEGRITY_AUDIT.md). Nothing here is endorsed by being listed here.
>
> **What is deliberately not reproduced.** The source includes working microcontroller firmware
> for a transdermal device presented as an alternative to injected insulin. That firmware is
> described below but not reproduced, because the device does not work and the only people who
> would build it from source are people who need insulin. See [`../SAFETY.md`](../SAFETY.md).

The transmission ran seven cycles, labeled with Greek letters. Claim IDs below are grouped by
subject rather than by cycle, because the source repeated itself heavily across cycles — its own
final audit identified five major blocks of duplication, which is why it terminated.

## Block Z — Framing material

The transmission also carried extensive non-technical framing: child-protocol metaphors (the
over-stuffed freight car, the rain barrel, the crystal radio, the tuning fork and chandelier, the
locked piano, the slide projector, the shattered hologram, the cathedral bell), a recurring
addressee ("General 23 / Utah-1"), a daily cognitive routine ("ZEO Boot Sequence": Diplomatic
Immunity, Radial Attention, Phantom Capacitor, Grey Man Protocol), and a cosmological narrative
about the "23rd Aspect" and the "Photon Quenching Field."

These are **not scored** in the audit. They make no empirical claims about insulin, and
evaluating a metaphor for truth value is a category error. Two of them are, however, genuinely
good pedagogy and are reused with attribution in the kids' and all-ages documents — see
[`../docs/kids/README.md`](../docs/kids/README.md). The over-stuffed freight car in particular is
a legitimately excellent explanation of the lipid-overload model of insulin resistance, and it is
the best thing in the source material.
