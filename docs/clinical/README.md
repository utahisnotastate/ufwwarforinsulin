## Quick reference card

### Triage, in order

1. **Type 1 or Type 2?** Determines whether this is a disagreement or an emergency.
2. **"Have you skipped or cut back any doses?"** — neutral phrasing gets a truthful answer.
3. **Are they injecting insulin not obtained from a pharmacy?**
4. **Symptoms now?** Polyuria, polydipsia, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, Kussmaul
   respiration, fruity breath, altered mental status.

### Act immediately if

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| T1D + any insulin reduction or omission | **DKA pathway.** Glucose, β-hydroxybutyrate, VBG, electrolytes **now** |
| On SGLT2i, or reduced carbohydrate intake | **Euglycemic DKA possible — a normal glucose does not exclude it** |
| Injecting self-made or non-pharmaceutical insulin | Stop. Replace with pharmaceutical supply same day. Screen for injection-site infection and sepsis. Consider anti-insulin antibodies if control is erratic |
| Using a device *instead of* insulin | Urgent endocrine involvement |
| Recurrent unexplained DKA | Ask directly about intentional omission; consider diabulimia; psychiatric referral |

### What they're right about — concede these explicitly

- Insulin resistance is lipid-driven (DAG → novel PKC → IRS-1), not a "broken receptor"
- Subcutaneous insulin is non-physiological; the liver should see it first
- C-peptide was wrongly dismissed as inert
- Contraction-mediated glucose uptake is insulin-signaling-independent
- **The price was indefensible, people rationed, and people died**

Conceding these is not a courtesy. It is what buys you credibility on the next section.

### What is wrong — the five facts worth carrying

| Their claim | The fact |
|---|---|
| A frequency device replaces insulin | No mechanism. **DKA in 12–48 h.** The frequency they cite doesn't come out of their own equation |
| C-peptide reverses microvascular disease | **Cebix Phase 2b, 2015, n=250: placebo beat drug.** Company dissolved 30 days after unblinding |
| Insulin therapy in T2D is an iatrogenic disaster | **ORIGIN: HR 1.04, neutral over 6.2 y. UKPDS 10-year follow-up showed mortality benefit** |
| Pumps/CGM are inferior transitional tech | **Registry n=13,922: hypoglycemic coma IRR 0.68 — lower with closed loop** |
| Home synthesis gives clinical-grade insulin for pennies | API alone costs $0.86/vial. The secreted species is a largely **inactive precursor** — likely failure mode is under-dosing → DKA |

### The conversation, in four moves

1. **Concede first.** "You're right that the subcutaneous route is unphysiological, and you're
   right that the price was indefensible."
2. **Separate the two diseases.** Much of the source is about Type 2 pathophysiology; much of
   the danger is to people with Type 1. Patients often don't realize it conflates them.
3. **Give a timeline, not a theory.** "Without insulin you'd be in hospital within two days."
4. **Don't argue the physics.** You can't win it in 12 minutes. One sentence — "the frequency
   they give doesn't come out of their own equation" — then point them at
   [the worked analysis](../technical/why-the-physics-layer-fails.md) and move on.

### Address cost in the room

Ask what they're paying. Then act:

- **$35/month cap** — Medicare Part D (from Jan 2023) and Part B (from July 2023)
- Manufacturer patient-assistance programs from all three major makers, including for the
  uninsured
- Unbranded biologics / authorized generics at lower cash price
- Community health center 340B pricing
- Walmart ReliOn human insulin (~$25/vial) as a fallback — but switching from analogues to
  NPH/regular is a real clinical task requiring timing education, not a handoff

A patient who leaves with affordable insulin is much less likely to return to a document
promising to make it for free.

### Emergency recognition for patient and family education

**DKA** — thirst, polyuria, nausea/vomiting, abdominal pain, deep rapid breathing, fruity
breath, confusion, drowsiness. Emergency.

**Severe hypoglycemia** — shakiness, sweating, confusion, slurred speech, incoordination,
seizure, unconsciousness. Conscious and swallowing → fast carbohydrate. Unconscious or seizing
→ **nothing by mouth**, glucagon if available, emergency services.

### Document

The specific beliefs stated, what the patient reports *doing* (distinct from believing), risks
explained, patient response, plan. If they decline recommended therapy, document capacity and
the specific risks discussed.
